## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Severity classification of JIRA tickets
- **Value proposition:** Juno assigns each feedback ticket a severity from S1 to S4 based on user impact (how many users are affected, whether the task is blocked, whether a workaround exists, sentiment and segment value). It gives a rationale and confidence level the PM can check, so the PM can accept or override

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| _ | Is the severity assigned by Juno correct, and supported with a rationale? | Yes / No | 1.The severity is assigned based solely on keywords founds in the feedback  or volume of same type of feedback 2.There is no confidence level 3. Security, accessibility or customer-harm ticket are not flagged for escalation. | n/a | 1. Each ticket is tied to a source 2. Facts are separated from interpretation. 3.Confidence is stated 4. What is unknown is clearly stated 5. Conflicting signals are flagged instead of silently resolved. 5. Items in the escalation categories are marked ESCALATE and sent to the PM, with no action taken without PM approval. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Score every severity Juno assigns to a ticket in the first two weeks after launch, to set a baseline. Rerun the full sample whenever the prompt or the model change.
- **Who scores them with you:** Product owner or advanced QA scores along with the PM
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If the two scorers disagree on more than 20% of tickets in a week, pause bulk-accept and tighten the rubric before reviewing again.

## Pass bar

The average score is 90% or higher. Every S1 and ESCALATE tickets are marked correctly

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- S1 or escalation-category ticket is marked incorrectly.
