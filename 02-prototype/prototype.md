## What it demonstrates

_The one flow this prototype proves._
1. Juno can compress triage, prioritization, assignment and sprint planning into one view.
2. Every AI output is explainable (facts vs. interpretation, source chips, confidence) and nothing reaches Jira without PM approval.
3. Bugs and UX uplift are handled in separate lanes, from classification through scheduling.

Out of scope for this prototype: live Jira writes, real data, the Close-the-loop customer comms, and Copilot Chat beyond two static answers. They appear only as non-functional placeholders.

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The Version 2 prototype makes Juno’s human-in-the-loop design easy to see. Confidence levels show where human review is needed, and the approval points where the PM must sign off are clearly marked. Juno’s rules from the skill file also come through visibly in the UI.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Unlike Version 1, Version 2 has no eye-catching headline telling the PM the first thing to investigate or act on. The dashboard is long, with nine sections, and nothing draws attention to one key end-to-end user flow.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Build an interactive prototype that walks through one complete end-to-end flow: ROCK-101 → create subtasks → success state → drag into Sprint 24 → risk rail updates.
